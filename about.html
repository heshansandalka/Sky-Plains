Our Vision

Lanka Nature is a digital initiative designed to document the unique avian biodiversity of Sri Lanka. Focusing on the misty highlands of Horton Plains, we capture the essence of rare and endemic bird species thriving in this unique cloud forest ecosystem.

Our platform serves as a community-driven database where enthusiasts can explore high-quality photography of endemic species like the Ceylon Blue Magpie.

Featured Endemic Birds

  • 🐦 Ceylon Blue Magpie - A vibrant forest dweller.
  • 🐦 Brahminy Kite - Often seen near coastal regions.
  • 🐦 Yellow-eared Bulbul - A highland specialty.
  • 🐦 Sri Lanka Blue Flycatcher - Found in the dense canopy.
